# Welcome to My Blog!

Hey there! I'm **Prodhosh VS**, a Full Stack Developer and AI Engineer currently pursuing CSE at VIT Chennai and Data Science at IIT Madras.

I've been wanting to start a blog for a while now — and here we are. This is where I'll share:

- **Tutorials** on React, Next.js, Python, and the tools I use every day
- **Deep dives** into AI/ML concepts and their real-world applications
- **Project breakdowns** — how I built various SaaS and open-source projects
- **Learnings** from hackathons, open-source contributions (GSSoC, NSoC), and internships
- **Thoughts** on the developer experience and the tech industry

## Why a Blog?

I've always believed that the best way to truly understand something is to explain it to others. Writing forces clarity. And if my learnings can help even one developer, that's a win.

## What to Expect

I'll try to ship **2-3 posts per month**. Topics will range from technical tutorials to opinion pieces on tech trends. I'll keep things practical and actionable — no fluff.

If you find something useful, share it. If you spot something wrong, reach out!

Happy reading. 🚀

— Prodhosh
